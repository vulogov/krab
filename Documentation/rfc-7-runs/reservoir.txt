Forward-secrecy window is bounded below by MAX_TTL
  chunk N decrypts objects whose tag epoch is N
  such an object may arrive up to MAX_TTL/EPOCH = 45 epochs later
  => chunk N MUST survive until N+45; live chunks = 45
  => seizure at T exposes epochs T-45 d .. T, whatever EPOCH is
  live reservoir state: 45 x 32 B = 1440 B

Reservoir sizing (32 B/epoch, 1-day epochs)
  one credential term (90 d)      2880 B     1.2 QR codes at EC-M
  one year                       11680 B     5.0 QR codes at EC-M
  five years                     58400 B    25.1 QR codes at EC-M

  The ratchet -- reservoir_{n+1} = HKDF(reservoir_n || DH(fresh)) -- means
  a reservoir need only span the maximum interval between contacts, not a
  lifetime. A quantum adversary recovers DH(fresh) but not reservoir_n, so
  PQ security survives provided the ROOT of the chain was PQ-established.

Reservoir against per-message hybrid (280-byte message)
  per-message hybrid surcharge      3072 B/message (4096 vs 1024 bucket)
  reservoir setup, one hybrid KEM   4096 B, once per correspondent
  crossover                         1.33 messages
  after   10 messages: reservoir   4096 B against hybrid    30720 B  (    8x)
  after  100 messages: reservoir   4096 B against hybrid   307200 B  (   75x)
  after 1000 messages: reservoir   4096 B against hybrid  3072000 B  (  750x)

  A hybrid-KEM-established reservoir pays for itself before the second
  message. RFC 3 §11.1 concedes remote peering is the common case, so the
  reservoir must be reachable without a physical meeting or Krab has no
  post-quantum story for most correspondents.
